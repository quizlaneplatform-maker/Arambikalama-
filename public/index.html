From blank page to game night

Four steps, and most teachers are done before the bell.

  1. Write the questions

    Type them yourself, or give AI a topic and edit what it drafts.

  2. Share the code

    Students join on any phone or laptop with a 6-digit code. No accounts.

  3. Play

    Fast answers and streaks score bonus points. The answer is revealed once everyone has answered.

  4. Read the report

    See who got what, and which question the class found hardest.

Two ways to play

Live game

The whole class answers the same question at the same time, on your big screen.

  • A countdown on every question
  • A leaderboard after each reveal
  • A podium for the top three

Self-paced

Students work through the quiz on their own while you watch their progress.

  • Good for homework and revision
  • Late joiners can still play
  • Live progress for every student
